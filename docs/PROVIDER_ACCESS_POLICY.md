# Provider access policy

Magic Screen is a personal research workflow. Source access must follow each provider's current terms, not just the app owner's personal-use intent.

## Musaffa and HalalScreener

- The app requires separate status records from both providers.
- AI research is optional and external. The user copies the ticker list and prompt into a chosen assistant.
- The assistant may use a provider only if that provider's current terms permit the specific AI/automated access method.
- Do not bypass sign-in, paywalls, CAPTCHA, rate limits, access controls or technical restrictions.
- Do not scrape, crawl, bulk-download or automate collection where the provider prohibits it.
- If permitted access is unclear or unavailable, return `No data` and ask for a manual check.
- Never guess a status, financial ratio, methodology, period, date or source URL.
- Missing numeric fields must remain `null`.

A fresh Halal result from both providers is required for an eligible decision. A result from one provider cannot substitute for the other.

## Investing Paths

Investing Paths observations are manual only. The owner opens the page and records visible rank, EY, ROC, fiscal period, checked date and direct source URL. Do not scrape, automate browser access, bulk-download or use an AI assistant to collect its rankings.

## Data use

- Personal research only.
- Do not publish or redistribute provider data as a public feed or commercial service without permission.
- Re-check current provider terms before changing the workflow.
- AI-generated research is evidence to review, not a fatwa or investment recommendation.
