## UPWK — Upwork Inc.

**FY ended:** 31 December 2025  
**Filing accession:** 0001627475-26-000012  
**SEC filing:** https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1627475/000162747526000012/upwk-20251231.htm  
**Primary issue:** marketable-securities cash treatment and customer funds

### FY2025 evidence

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Reported operating income | 129.307 |
| Cash and cash equivalents | 294.356 |
| Marketable securities | 378.425 |
| Restricted cash | 3.800 |
| Customer escrow and funds in transit | 180.752 |
| Convertible-note carrying value, current | 359.770 |

The marketable-securities portfolio includes money-market funds, commercial paper, Treasury bills, corporate bonds, U.S. government securities, asset-backed securities and foreign-government/entity debt securities. The reviewed disclosure does not provide a complete instrument-by-instrument amount and maturity breakdown. The company describes the securities as available for current operations, but this is not sufficient to classify the entire portfolio as unrestricted cash equivalents under a uniform MFI policy.

The $3.8m restricted cash balance is separate. Customer escrow and funds in transit of $180.752m are matched by escrow funds payable and must not be counted as unrestricted cash. The $359.770m convertible-note carrying value is current at the FY2025 reporting date.

### Ranking sensitivity

| Scenario | EY | Change vs baseline |
|---|---:|---:|
| Current candidate policy: cash equivalents only | 11.13% | — |
| Marketable securities treated as cash-like | 16.52% | +5.38 percentage points |

The sensitivity improves the earnings yield materially, but does not justify adopting the policy. The whole securities portfolio must be assessed using the same maturity, credit-quality, liquidity, restriction and settlement criteria applied to every issuer.

**Reviewer decisions**
1. Approve or reject a uniform policy for marketable securities; do not make an issuer-specific exception.
2. Keep restricted cash and customer escrow/in-transit outside unrestricted cash.
3. Confirm that the convertible-note carrying value is the annual FY2025 input and that no aggregate/component double count exists.
4. Keep the current 11.13% EY as a review-only output until cash policy approval.

The FY2025 cash-and-equivalents balance includes money-market funds, U.S. Treasury securities and commercial paper. The convertible notes matured and were repaid in June 2026, after the FY2025 reporting date. That later repayment must not overwrite the FY2025 annual debt snapshot used in this ranking.

### Invested-capital sensitivity

| Scenario | ROC | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Current candidate formula | 278.51% | Current assets - current liabilities + net PP&E |
| Net-PP&E-only diagnostic | 2,890.32% | Removes working-capital component; not an approved formula |

The net-PP&E-only sensitivity produces an extreme ROC and materially changes ordinal ranks. It is diagnostic evidence that the denominator definition matters, not a reason to change the current formula for IRWD alone.

**Reviewer decisions**
1. Preserve FY2025 debt at $584.680m unless a newer annual filing is selected for the entire annual snapshot.
2. Reconcile the convertible-note carrying value and revolver amount to the accession-specific debt note.
3. Keep the cash and equivalents balance separate from any securities that do not meet the common cash-equivalent definition.
4. Do not adopt net-PP&E-only ROC. Any denominator alternative must be applied to the full cohort and tested against negative working capital and lease/ROU scope.

**Primary issue:** exceptional operating revenue quality and denominator sensitivity

### FY2025 EBIT evidence

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Collaboration-arrangement revenue | 53.737 |
| Total operating expenses | (36.835) |
| **Reported operating income** | **16.902** |

The filing explains that the $53.737m collaboration-arrangement revenue arose from settlement of the customer refund liability following the Amgen amendment. QTTB has not commercialized product candidates and does not expect product sales for several years, if at all. The reported $16.902m operating-income subtotal is arithmetically valid GAAP operating income, but it may not be a stable recurring EBIT proxy.

A mechanical diagnostic that removes the exceptional collaboration revenue gives:

[
16.902 - 53.737 = -36.835
]

This is not an approved normalized EBIT value and must not replace the reported operating-income subtotal without a documented, cohort-wide exceptional-item policy.
