The marketable-securities portfolio includes money-market funds, commercial paper, Treasury bills, corporate bonds, U.S. government securities, asset-backed securities and debt securities issued by foreign governments or entities. Contractual maturities are within 36 months from purchase, and the securities are classified available-for-sale. The company says the portfolio is available for current operations, but this does not establish that every instrument is equivalent to unrestricted cash. Instrument-level amounts and maturities are not sufficiently disaggregated in the reviewed disclosure to apply a consistent haircut or cash-like percentage.

Customer funds held in escrow are held in demand or interest-bearing accounts at U.S. financial institutions and licensed money transmitters. The company is required to hold them in trust and records a corresponding escrow funds payable liability. Do not deduct the $180.752m escrow asset from EV as unrestricted cash or add the payable as conventional debt.

The $359.770m note carrying amount is current at 31 December 2025 because the notes matured on 15 August 2026. The filing's debt note reconciles $360.998m principal less $1.228m unamortized issuance costs to $359.770m carrying value. Later repayment or conversion is not substituted into the FY2025 annual snapshot.

### UPWK decisions

1. Keep the $378.425m securities portfolio outside unrestricted cash until a uniform maturity, credit, liquidity and instrument-level policy is approved.
2. Exclude $3.8m restricted cash and $180.752m customer escrow from unrestricted cash.
3. Retain $359.770m current debt for the FY2025 snapshot; do not use post-year-end maturity outcomes to rewrite the annual figure.
4. Decide whether any portion of marketable securities qualifies as cash-like only with instrument-level evidence and a cohort-wide rule.

Use the cash-equivalent balance as the candidate subject to the common cash policy.

### Annual versus subsequent-period boundary

The convertible notes matured on 15 June 2026 and were repaid in June 2026 using cash on hand. This is subsequent-period context only. For FY2025 annual MFI calculations, preserve the $199.680m current carrying value and the $385m revolver outstanding at the FY2025 reporting date. Do not blend Q2/Q3 2026 balances into the FY2025 annual financial snapshot.

### IRWD decisions

1. Keep $199.680m notes and $385m revolver as distinct FY2025 debt instruments; no double counting of facility commitment.
2. Preserve the FY2025 annual snapshot despite later note repayment.
3. Confirm the $215.456m cash candidate and apply the same cash-equivalent policy to all issuers.
4. Review the small $35.368m invested-capital denominator and the ROU/lease policy before production approval.

## QTTB — Q32 Bio Inc.

**Form 10-K:** FY ended 31 December 2025  
**Accession:** 0001193125-26-099339  
**Filing:** https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1661998/000119312526099339/qttb-20251231.htm

### EBIT and ranking-quality risk

- GAAP income from operations: **$16.902m**, directly reported.
- Collaboration-arrangement revenue: **$53.737m**
- Total operating expenses: **$36.835m**
- Candidate EBIT: 53.737 - 36.835 = **$16.902m**

The reported operating subtotal is arithmetically correct. However, the filing explains that the $53.737m collaboration-arrangement revenue arose from settlement of the customer refund liability following the Amgen amendment. It is a nonrecurring settlement item, not ordinary recurring product sales. The filing also states Q32 has not commercialized product candidates and does not expect product sales for several years, if at all.

This means the $16.902m EBIT candidate, and therefore its positive EY/ROC and tied shadow rank, is materially affected by a one-time operating revenue item. Do not silently remove the amount or change the formula. The accounting owner must decide whether the standard MFI numerator uses reported GAAP operating income without adjustment, or whether a documented cohort-wide exceptional-item policy is required. Until that decision is made, QTTB's rank should be read as a reported-GAAP mechanical result, not a recurring-earnings quality signal.
