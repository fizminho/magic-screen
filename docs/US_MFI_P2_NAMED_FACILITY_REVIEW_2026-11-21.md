## MNTN — MNTN, Inc.

**FY ended:** 31 December 2025  
**Accession:** 0001891027-26-000019  
**Filing:** https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1891027/000189102726000019/mntn-20251231.htm

### Convertible notes and holdback note

The 2023 convertible notes had $47.1m original principal. At the May 23, 2025 IPO, $23.1m principal plus $3.2m accrued interest converted into shares; $24.0m principal plus $3.1m accrued interest was repaid in cash; and a further $24.0m principal converted into shares under the amended settlement. The filing states that all principal and accrued interest were fully settled in connection with the IPO and the December 31, 2025 outstanding balance was zero.

The $0.6m QuickFrame acquisition holdback short-term note payable at December 31, 2024 was settled in Q1 2025. The remaining balance at December 31, 2025 was zero.

### Revolver and employee note receivable

The December 8, 2025 amended financing agreement provided up to $50m of asset-based revolving borrowings, with an option to request up to a further $30m. At December 31, 2025, the company reported no outstanding borrowings and $49.4m of availability. The facility is secured by substantially all company assets and requires at least $75m of depository and operating accounts with Western Alliance Bank, subject to exceptions.

One partial-recourse promissory note from an employee remained outstanding at year-end. It relates to an early exercise of stock options and is an amount owed **to the company**. The balance-sheet line for notes receivable from employees is $0.181m. It is an asset, not company borrowing.

### Review disposition

- Convertible notes: settled; year-end balance zero.
- QuickFrame holdback note: settled in Q1 2025; year-end balance zero.
- Revolver: $0 drawn; $49.4m available. Facility-specific zero only.
- Employee partial-recourse note: $0.181m receivable asset; exclude from debt.
- Keep the $75m bank-account covenant visible in the cash-availability review.
- Do not infer that all debt categories are zero solely from the named facilities; retain the broader note-completeness check until the accounting reviewer closes it.

An outstanding $28.4m letter of credit under the facility supported a sublease.
