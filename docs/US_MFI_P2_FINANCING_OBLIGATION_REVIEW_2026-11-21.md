It is a strong candidate for debt-like EV inclusion at its carrying amount, subject to a uniform policy for royalty monetizations. Do not put it into the conventional-debt fact field or double count it with any aggregate debt total.

3. Keep lease liabilities separate from royalty financing.
4. Do not adjust reported EBIT ad hoc. If any policy adjusts EBIT for royalty economics, it must be defined cohort-wide and applied consistently.

## PRDO — Perdoceo Education Corporation

**FY ended:** 31 December 2025  
**Accession:** 0001193125-26-059331  
**Filing:** https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046568/000119312526059331/prdo-20251231.htm

### Reconciled amounts and mechanics

| Item | FY2025 | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Failed sale-leaseback financing liability | 56.992 | Noncurrent balance-sheet carrying amount |
| Prior-year construction financing liability | 56.500 | Reclassified when the lease commenced |
| FY2025 principal payments | 0.489 | Financing cash flow |
| FY2025 interest expense | 5.400 | Failed sale-leaseback interest |
| FY2025 amortization expense | 1.200 | Depreciation/amortization on retained asset |
| Future contractual payments | 154.067 | Undiscounted contractual schedule |
| 2026 scheduled payments | 5.043 | Next-12-month contractual cash payments |

The transaction relates to the new St. Augustine campus at USAHS. Construction commenced in April 2023 under a build-to-suit arrangement. Perdoceo was deemed the owner during construction and recorded the landlord's contributions as construction in progress with a corresponding financing liability. The arrangement commenced as a lease in January 2025 with a stated 25-year term.

At commencement, Perdoceo concluded it failed sale-leaseback accounting because it retained control of the asset and the risks and rewards of ownership did not transfer to the landlord. It retained the asset on the balance sheet and depreciates it. The financing obligation was initially recognized at $56.5m net proceeds. Monthly payments are recorded as interest expense and reduction of the liability, not rent expense.

The balance-sheet carrying amount of $56.992m is not the same as the $154.067m undiscounted future payment schedule. Do not substitute the contractual total for the carrying value in EV.

### Accounting interpretation

This is economically financing-like: the company retains the campus asset, records depreciation, recognizes interest expense, and reduces a financing liability as payments are made. It is not an ordinary operating lease liability. It is also distinct from the separately disclosed finance and operating lease balances.

**Candidate MFI treatment:** include the $56.992m carrying amount in a separate failed-sale-leaseback financing bucket, with debt-like EV treatment as the leading candidate. Keep the $154.067m future payment schedule as disclosure evidence, not as an additional debt amount.

**Open review points**
1. Confirm the exact current/noncurrent split from the detailed note; the face balance sheet presents the $56.992m liability as noncurrent.
2. Confirm the asset and depreciation treatment is consistently reflected in the EBIT numerator and invested-capital denominator.
3. Do not add the $56.992m again as an ordinary lease liability.
4. Keep ordinary operating and finance lease liabilities separate until the global lease policy is approved.

## VXRT — Vaxart

**FY ended:** 31 December 2025  
**Accession:** 0001437749-26-008023  
**Filing:** https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/72444/000143774926008023/vxrt20251231_10k.htm

### Reconciled amounts and mechanics

| Item | FY2025 | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Liability from sale of future royalties, current | 1.381 | Current balance-sheet portion |
| Liability from sale of future royalties, noncurrent | 2.679 | Noncurrent balance-sheet portion |
| **Total liability** | **4.060** | Current + noncurrent |
| Non-cash interest expense | 2.823 | Imputed interest on unamortized liability |
| Non-cash royalty revenue remitted to HCRP | 4.521 | Reduces the liability |
| Opening liability | 5.758 | FY2025 roll-forward opening balance |
| Closing liability | **4.060** | Opening less royalties plus imputed interest |

The agreement dates to April 2016, when Aviragen sold certain Inavir royalty rights in Japan to HealthCare Royalty Partners III, L.P. for $20.0m. HCRP receives the first $3.0m plus any cumulative shortfall and 15% of the next $1.0m in royalties for the first interest period. From 1 April 2025 through 24 December 2029, HCRP receives the first $2.7m plus cumulative shortfalls and 15% of the next $1.0m, subject to the contract's specified period exclusions. The cumulative shortfall was $4.4m at 31 December 2025.

Importantly, Vaxart states it is **not obligated to pay HCRP beyond the royalties it receives from Daiichi Sankyo**. Any remaining shortfall after 24 December 2029 is paid only for so long as Vaxart continues to receive the relevant royalties. The liability nevertheless accretes imputed interest, and the company continues to record the related royalty revenue until the liability and related interest are fully amortized.

### Accounting interpretation

This is a financing claim against a specified royalty stream with imputed-interest accretion, but the explicit limit to royalties received makes it different from ordinary recourse debt. The $4.060m carrying value should remain separately visible with a limited-recourse/royalty-stream flag.

**Candidate MFI treatment:** consider inclusion in debt-like EV at carrying value under a policy that includes monetized royalty claims, while retaining the non-recourse limitation as a separate attribute. This is a policy decision, not a conventional-debt classification. Do not treat the $1.929m royalty revenue as free cash flow available to equity without considering the associated remittance and liability amortization.

**Open review points**
1. Confirm the legal interpretation of the no-payment-beyond-received-royalties clause and any exceptions.
2. Confirm that the full royalty stream and cumulative shortfall are not already reflected in another liability.
3. Preserve current and noncurrent components; do not infer the liability is extinguished after the stated royalty interest period without the contractual shortfall clause.
4. Keep operating lease liabilities separate.

## Cross-issuer policy recommendation for accounting-owner review

A consistent MFI EV policy should distinguish the following buckets rather than flattening all obligations into a single generic debt fact:

1. Conventional borrowings: bank facilities, notes and term loans.
2. Royalty monetization financing: BCRX and VXRT.
3. Failed sale-leaseback financing: PRDO.
4. Finance lease liabilities.
5. Operating lease liabilities.
6. Other financing obligations (e.g. tax receivable agreements, if policy determines they are debt-like).

**Recommended candidate rule:** an obligation belongs in a separate debt-like EV bucket when it represents proceeds previously received in exchange for a contractual or economically enforceable claim on future operating cash flows/assets and the filing records a financing liability or effective-interest accretion. For limited-recourse royalty transactions, preserve a separate limited-recourse attribute rather than treating them as ordinary borrowings. Include the carrying amount, not undiscounted future payments, and never double count a balance already included in conventional debt.

This rule is a proposed policy for human review only. It must be tested against the whole active cohort, approved once, and applied consistently. It must not be selectively applied because it changes an issuer's EY or rank.
