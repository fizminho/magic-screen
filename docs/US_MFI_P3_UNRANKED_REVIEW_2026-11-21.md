The debt note describes a $10m term-loan face value, with $0.3m unamortized discount and unaccreted end-of-term charge, producing a carrying value of approximately $9.7m. The maturity schedule is $0.366m in 2026, $4.597m in 2027 and $5.037m in 2028, reconciling to $10m principal. The resolver's $0.366m current plus $9.323m noncurrent equals $9.689m carrying value.

## BMBL — Bumble Inc.

**FY ended:** 31 December 2025  
**Accession:** 0001830043-26-000027  
**Filing:** https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1830043/000183004326000027/bmbl-20251231.htm

The filing reports operating loss of **$805.778m**. Total revenue was $965.658m. The operating statement includes a $1,039.027m impairment-loss line. The filing separately discloses 2025 goodwill impairment charges of $396.3m in Q4 following a sustained stock-price decline and $258.1m in Q2 following a revised outlook/strategic shift. The two disclosed goodwill charges total $654.4m; the total impairment line is larger and must not be casually equated to only those two goodwill charges.

A purely mechanical add-back of the entire impairment line would produce:

[
-805.778 + 1,039.027 = 233.249
]

This is a diagnostic sensitivity only. It is not an approved normalized EBIT, and the entire impairment line must not be assumed to qualify for exclusion without a complete note review and an exceptional-item policy applied consistently across the cohort.

The company reported $50m available under the revolving credit facility. Facility availability is not drawn debt.
