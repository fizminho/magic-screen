### EBIT and invested capital

- GAAP income from operations: **$225.7m**. This is a directly reported operating subtotal, not the $445.9m non-GAAP Adjusted Operating Income.
- Current assets: **$454.0m**
- Current liabilities: **$632.9m**
- Net PP&E: **$162.6m**
- Candidate invested capital: 454.0 - 632.9 + 162.6 = **-$16.3m**

Current liabilities include **$474.6m current unearned revenue**, $115.1m accrued expenses and other current liabilities, $6.0m current operating lease liabilities and $5.9m current debt. Unearned revenue is a material operating liability; its treatment should be consistent across subscription/software issuers and should not be reclassified as conventional borrowing.

### GTM decisions

1. Keep invested capital negative and ROC unranked pending a cohort-wide working-capital policy.

3. Decide treatment of tax receivable agreement obligations in EV; do not silently classify as conventional debt or ignore them.
4. Keep short-term investments and restricted cash separate from unrestricted cash.
5. Decide lease-liability / ROU treatment consistently.

### EBIT and invested capital

- GAAP earnings from operations: **$3,174m**, directly reported in the consolidated statements of earnings. Do not substitute segment totals or a non-GAAP adjusted metric.
- Current assets: **$22,453m**
- Current liabilities: **$29,258m**
- Net PP&E: **$3,049m**
- Candidate invested capital: 22,453 - 29,258 + 3,049 = **-$3,756m**

Negative working capital is **-$6,805m**. Current liabilities include **$18,051m accounts payable**, $845m notes payable and short-term borrowings, and $10,362m other current liabilities. The candidate denominator is negative even after including $3,049m net PP&E. Do not force a positive denominator or cap ROC.

### Cash and debt scope

The prior register described the cash line as combined with restricted cash. That is incorrect: the 10-K separately reports **$3,690m cash and cash equivalents** and **$15m restricted cash**. The restricted cash relates to amounts collected and held on behalf of a third party for trade receivables previously sold.

| Item | FY2025 |
|---|---:|
| Cash and cash equivalents | 3,690 |
| Restricted cash | 15 |
| Notes payable and short-term borrowings | 845 |
| Long-term debt | 8,821 |
| Total debt | 9,666 |

The filing says cash equivalents include highly liquid investments with original maturities of three months or less. The unrestricted cash line can therefore be identified separately from restricted cash. The denominator, not the cash line, is the main blocker.

### HPQ decisions

1. Keep negative invested capital unranked.
2. Decide whether HP's large trade-payable balance should remain in operating current liabilities under the same cohort-wide formula.
3. Confirm conventional debt carrying-value treatment and do not add total debt to its current/noncurrent components.
4. Use $3,690m as the cash candidate only subject to the global cash policy; do not include the $15m restricted amount.

## XPOF — Xponential Fitness, Inc.

**Form 10-K:** FY ended 31 December 2025  
**Accession:** 0001802156-26-000016

### EBIT and invested capital

- GAAP operating income: **$19.848m**, directly reported.
- Current assets: **$94.646m**
- Current liabilities: **$115.975m**
- Net PP&E: **$10.891m**
- Candidate invested capital: 94.646 - 115.975 + 10.891 = **-$10.438m**

Negative working capital is **-$21.329m**, exceeding net PP&E. The candidate denominator is therefore negative. Keep ROC unranked until the denominator policy is resolved.

### Cash, debt, leases and NCI

The balance sheet presents **$45.863m cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash** together. The liquidity note clarifies that cash and cash equivalents are **$33.7m**, excluding **$12.2m restricted cash**. Restricted cash consists of $11.4m marketing-fund restricted cash and a standby-letter-of-credit guarantee. Use the $33.7m candidate, not the combined balance, subject to the global cash policy.

| Item | FY2025 | Treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| Cash and cash equivalents | 33.7 | Cash candidate from liquidity note |
| Restricted cash | 12.2 | Exclude from unrestricted cash |
| Current portion of long-term debt | 5.250 | Current debt |
| Long-term debt, net | 500.500 | Noncurrent debt carrying amount |
| Total balance-sheet debt | 505.750 | Current plus noncurrent carrying amounts |
| Principal payments due | 525.000 | Contractual principal schedule; not the carrying value |
| Operating lease ROU assets | 13.736 | Separate lease asset |
| Noncurrent lease liabilities | 14.243 | Separate lease liability; current lease portion is within current liabilities |
| Redeemable convertible preferred stock | 0 | None outstanding at 31 December 2025 |
| Noncontrolling interest | 28% ownership | XPO Inc. consolidates XPO LLC but holds 72%; EV treatment requires policy |

The debt note discloses $1.538m unamortized issuance costs and $18.626m unamortized original issue discount, both presented as reductions to long-term debt. The current $5.250m plus noncurrent $500.500m carrying values total $505.750m, while the principal schedule is $525.000m. The precise bridge between the current portion, noncurrent carrying value and unamortized deductions needs a note-level tie-out before debt is approved.

The company repurchased all outstanding convertible preferred shares on 8 December 2025; the year-end balance sheet reports none outstanding. Do not carry forward the prior-year $116.810m mezzanine-equity balance. XPO Inc. consolidates XPO LLC and reports noncontrolling interests representing 28% of the LLC's economic ownership. Decide EV treatment consistently with consolidated EBIT.

### XPOF decisions

1. Keep negative invested capital and ROC unranked.
2. Use $33.7m as the cash candidate, not $45.863m, while retaining the $12.2m restricted amount separately.
3. Reconcile $525m principal to $505.750m balance-sheet carrying amounts before approving debt.
4. Decide whether to add NCI to EV because the operating results are consolidated while XPO Inc. owns 72% of XPO LLC.
5. Do not include the repurchased preferred shares as year-end debt or equity claims.
