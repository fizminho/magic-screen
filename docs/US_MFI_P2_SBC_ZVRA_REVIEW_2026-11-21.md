The FY2025 balance sheet presents the debt as long-term. The filing states that the loan matures on the fifth anniversary of the April 5, 2024 closing date. It is secured by a first-priority lien on substantially all current and future assets and certain subsidiary guarantors. Events of default can accelerate the obligations.

The company also describes two separate instruments that had already been extinguished before FY2025 year-end: the $5m Nantahala secured note was repaid in April 2024, and the Wells Fargo margin account was repaid in April 2024 with margin capabilities removed. Do not add either to FY2025 debt.

### Review disposition

- **Debt principal:** $63.608m.

- **Optional tranches:** $40m not drawn before expiry; exclude from debt.
- **Security / acceleration:** first-priority lien over substantially all assets and customary acceleration rights.
- **Subsequent events:** later 2026 repayment does not rewrite FY2025 annual MFI inputs.
- **Candidate EV input:** $61.928m FY2025 carrying value, subject to the global debt policy.
